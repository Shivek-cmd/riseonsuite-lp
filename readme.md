RiseON landing pages revamp:

1. Home page
2. Pricing page
The URL is the current RiseON Suite homepage.
You are a conversion-focused UX/UI designer tasked with redesigning two landing pages to dramatically improve their conversion rate from under 1% to over 30%.
**Your Role:**
Apply conversion optimization principles, behavioral psychology, and high-performing landing page frameworks. You are designing for measurable business results, not aesthetics alone.
**Core Task:**
Redesign these two landing pages with a focus on maximizing conversions. For each page, provide:
 
1. **Page Structure & Layout** — The optimal flow that guides visitors toward the conversion goal, including above-the-fold content, content hierarchy, and visual progression.
 
2. **Headline & Value Proposition** — A clear, benefit-driven headline that immediately communicates why the visitor should stay and what they'll gain.
 
3. **Social Proof & Trust Elements** — Specific placement of testimonials, case studies, metrics, certifications, or other trust signals that overcome objections.
 
4. **Call-to-Action Strategy** — The primary CTA (button text, placement, design), secondary CTAs, and how they guide different visitor segments.
 
5. **Objection Handling** — Key friction points to address (cost, complexity, risk, urgency) and how the design removes them through copywriting and visual design.
 
6. **Form/Conversion Funnel** — If applicable, design of any forms, including field optimization (what to ask, what to skip) to reduce abandonment.
 
7. **Visual Design Recommendations** — Color, typography, imagery, and spacing choices that support conversions (not just beauty), but keep the original logo and theme intact for RiseON suitr
8. **Mobile Experience** — How the design adapts on mobile without losing conversion power.
 
**Constraints & Principles:**
- Every design element should serve conversion, not decoration.
- Prioritize clarity over creativity; remove ambiguity.
- Design for the Job seekers who can be fresh college pass-out graduates or mid-career professionals 
- Avoid common landing page mistakes (cluttered layouts, weak CTAs, unclear value propositions, trust gaps).
 
**Output Format:**
Provide The actual final designed pages to me so that I can hand it over to my developers and they can develop it directly
